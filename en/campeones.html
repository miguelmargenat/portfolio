Campeones

The challenge was never just to launch a bike with graphics inspired by the World Cup. The conversation was always about something else: how to convey the collective pride and emotion we all lived through in such an Argentine way.

That's where product design gets interesting: when it can also help build an emotional and cultural identity, not just something functional. Together with the design studio Pileta, we turned all that intent into a clear identity with plenty of character.
